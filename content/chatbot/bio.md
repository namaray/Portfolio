# About Me

## Who I Am
I'm Namare Shakib Angkon, a final-year Computer Science and Engineering student at United International University in Dhaka, Bangladesh, graduating in November 2026. I work where human-computer interaction meets hardware and AI: I build accessible technology, such as smart glasses for people with visual impairments and a portable Braille printer, and I test it with the people who will use it.

## How I Work
Most of my projects start with a conversation, not code.
1. **Listen**: focus groups and interviews with the people the project is for. For ARGUS and the Braille printer I planned and moderated focus groups with visually impaired participants.
2. **Define**: turn what they said into requirements, using thematic analysis (Taguette) and written specifications.
3. **Build**: whatever the project needs, from perception pipelines on a Jetson Orin Nano and stepper-motor rigs on a Raspberry Pi to 3D-printed housings and the web apps around them.
4. **Test**: usability studies, then back to listening.

## What I Care About
- **Accessibility and impact**: technology is most useful when it is built with, not just for, the people who are usually left out of the spec.
- **Hardware and software together**: the best assistive devices need both the code and the physical device to work well.
- **Honest evaluation**: real users, real studies, and not overstating results.
